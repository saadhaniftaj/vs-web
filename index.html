Case Studies

Proven Legacy Transformation

Real-world results achieved for massive distribution and manufacturing operations.

German Spare Parts Manufacturer

Legacy Knowledge Modernization

A massive, established manufacturing firm was struggling with severely outdated documentation processes, relying on physical and highly disorganized legacy engineering manuals.

The Solution: We engineered a custom, centralized search and documentation platform that digitized over 150,000+ pages of legacy manuals. Decades of engineering knowledge became instantly accessible and searchable through a unified dashboard, drastically cutting down engineering retrieval time.

Ohio Fulfillment Center

Workflow Consolidation & OCR

A mid-market fulfillment center was bogged down by a highly manual logistics pipeline, forcing staff to manually type over 500 inconsistently formatted shipment sheets every single day.

The Solution: We engineered a bespoke automated data pipeline utilizing advanced OCR. We consolidated their disjointed ingestion process, entirely eliminating manual data entry. What previously took hours of tedious human intervention was reduced to minutes of automated processing.
