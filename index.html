Case Studies

Proven Legacy Transformation

Real-world results achieved for massive distribution and manufacturing operations.

German Spare Parts Manufacturer

How do I digitize thousands of pages of engineering manuals?

The Solution: VanguardArc built a purpose-built internal search platform that instantly digitized and indexed over 150,000 pages of legacy manuals. Engineers now type a query and get the exact specification in seconds, from anywhere.

The Context: A long-established manufacturer had decades of product engineering manuals spread across physical binders and legacy systems. Finding a specific spec meant calling the right person and hoping they remembered. We solved this permanently.

Ohio Fulfillment Center

How to automate disjointed logistics pipelines and shipment ingestion.

The Solution: VanguardArc built an automated ingestion pipeline that reads and extracts data from 500 daily shipment sheets, entirely eliminating manual entry.

The Context: Staff were spending hours every day manually transcribing incoming shipment documents into their system—each one formatted differently, each one prone to human error. Processing that took hours now takes minutes.
